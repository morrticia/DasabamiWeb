The grape

Mtsvane means “green” in Georgian. Mtsvane Kakhuri is an aromatic white variety from Kakheti, long grown and blended alongside Rkatsiteli in eastern Georgia.

Made on its skins in qvevri, Mtsvane gives an amber wine that is often described as floral, with stone fruit, citrus and a mineral edge.

How Dasabami makes it

Dasabami’s Mtsvane grows among the family’s old vines in Tamarisi; the vineyard registry lists both Mtsvane Kakhuri and Mtsvane Goruli. Each recent vintage spent about six months on its skins and then three to eight months in qvevri.

Lots are small, 360 to 400 bottles, and every bottle is unfined, unfiltered and made with no added SO₂.

Questions

Mtsvane

What is Dasabami Mtsvane?

An amber natural wine from the Mtsvane grape, made by the Darsavelidze family in Tamarisi, Kvemo Kartli, Georgia, fermented on its skins in qvevri with ambient yeasts.

How long does Dasabami Mtsvane stay on its skins?

About six months, then three to eight months in qvevri: 2019 six months, 2021 eight months and 2022 three months in qvevri.

How many bottles of Dasabami Mtsvane are made?

360 to 400 bottles per vintage: 360 in 2019 and 2021, 400 in 2022.

What food goes with amber Mtsvane?

Its floral, fresh style suits lighter dishes: grilled fish, herbs, fresh cheeses and pkhali, the Georgian vegetable and walnut pâtés.
