The grape

Rkatsiteli brings structure and lively acidity; Mtsvane adds aromatics. The two grapes have long been grown and blended together in eastern Georgia.

How Dasabami makes it

The 2024 spent three months on its skins and four months in qvevri, at 12% alcohol; 800 bottles were made, unfined, unfiltered and with no added SO₂.

A 2025 Rkatsiteli–Mtsvane has also been poured at 27 Wine Cellar & Shop in Tbilisi.

Questions

Rkatsiteli–Mtsvane

What is Dasabami Rkatsiteli–Mtsvane?

An amber natural wine blending 80% Rkatsiteli and 20% Mtsvane, made by the Darsavelidze family in Tamarisi, Kvemo Kartli, Georgia, in qvevri with ambient yeasts.

How is the Rkatsiteli–Mtsvane made?

The 2024 spent three months on its skins and four months in qvevri, and was bottled unfined, unfiltered and with no added SO₂ at 12% alcohol.

How many bottles are made?

800 bottles of the 2024.
