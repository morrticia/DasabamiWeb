Dasabami/Wines
Natural Georgian wines

The wines.

Four indigenous grapes from one family hectare in Tamarisi, Kvemo Kartli. Three become amber wines, one is red, and there is a Rkatsiteli–Mtsvane blend. All ferment in qvevri with ambient yeasts and are bottled unfined, unfiltered, with no added SO₂.

Amber wineRkatsiteliრქაწითელიGeorgia’s great white grape, made the old way: fermented on its skins in qvevri until it turns amber. From the family’s 70-year-old vines in Tamarisi.More about Rkatsiteli → Amber wineMtsvaneმწვანეAn aromatic white grape from eastern Georgia, made as an amber wine: about six months on its skins in qvevri, in lots of a few hundred bottles.More about Mtsvane → Amber wineKhikhviხიხვიA rare white grape from Kakheti, made as a deep amber wine. Planted at Dasabami around 2009, it gives the family’s most powerful wine.More about Khikhvi → Red wineTavkveriთავკვერიKartli’s own red grape and Dasabami’s only red: a short time on its skins, then months of rest in qvevri.More about Tavkveri → Amber blendRkatsiteli–Mtsvaneრქაწითელი–მწვანეThe classic eastern Georgian pairing: 80% Rkatsiteli and 20% Mtsvane, made as an amber wine in qvevri.More about Rkatsiteli–Mtsvane →
